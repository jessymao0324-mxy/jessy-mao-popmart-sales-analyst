Labubu tripled Pop Mart in 2025; growth reversed in Q3 2026

Revenue rose 185% to RMB 37.1bn in 2025 on THE MONSTERS (Labubu) and plush toys. H1 2026 still grew 24%, but Q3 2026 revenue fell 45–50% as the Labubu wave cooled.

Key insights

Click an insight to explore it
  1. One IP drove the boom. THE MONSTERS grew from RMB 0.37bn (2023) to 14.16bn (2025), 38% of revenue.
  2. Plush became the core product. Plush rose from 3% of revenue (2023) to 57% (H1 2026).
  3. Overseas peaked first. Americas grew 748% in 2025, then fell 17% in H1 2026 and 70–75% in Q3 2026.
  4. Twinkle Twinkle is the next engine. RMB 2.65bn in H1 2026 (+581%), now the #2 IP.

What the product shift means

  1. Plush carried the growth. Plush accounted for 66% of the 2025 revenue increase (RMB 15.9bn of 24.1bn).
  2. Margins rose with the mix. Gross margin climbed from 57.5% (2022) to 72.1% (2025), before easing to 69.7% in H1 2026.
  3. Figures stalled in 2026. Figure revenue grew 0.3% in H1 2026 while plush grew 60%, so the business leans further on one format.
  4. Other merchandise fell. Other IP products dropped 15.8% in H1 2026.
